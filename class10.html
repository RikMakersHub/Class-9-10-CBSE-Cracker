📐 Mathematics Reference Suite

Basics (Board Crackers): RD Sharma or RS Aggarwal Class 10. Perfect for mastering every single variant of textbook algorithms and standard proofs.

Advanced (Olympiad/IOQM Core): Challenge and Thrill of Pre-College Mathematics (Krishnamurthy) & An Excursion in Mathematics. Dive straight into non-routine combinatorics, number theory, and advanced geometry algorithms.

⚡ Physics Concepts Suite

Basics (Board Crackers): Lakhmir Singh & Manjit Kaur Class 10. Excellent visual explanations and structured question sets for scoring 100% in board theory.

Advanced (JEE/NEET Foundation): Concepts of Physics by HC Verma (Volume 1). Read the mechanics and optics chapters early to build world-class engineering logic.

🧪 Chemistry Frameworks

Basics (Board Crackers): S. Chand Class 10 Chemistry. Keeps your chemical reactions balancing layout simple and clear.

Advanced (Competitive Logic): Pearson IIT Foundation Series Chemistry Class 10. Perfect for early exposure to structural bonding and mole concepts.

🌱 Biology Master Resources

Basics (Board Crackers): Selina Concise Biology or S. Chand Class 10. Great diagrams for physiological mapping.

Advanced (Olympiad Core): Campbell Biology (Concepts & Connections). The golden standard for students aiming for national-level Olympiad foundations.
