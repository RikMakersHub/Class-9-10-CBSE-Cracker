📐 Mathematics Reference Suite (Class 9)

Basics (Board Crackers): RD Sharma or RS Aggarwal Class 9. Crucial for solidifying foundational school proofs, coordinate mapping, and polynomial algorithms.

Advanced (Olympiad/IOQM Prep): Pre-College Mathematics (AIF) & Pathfinder for Olympiad Mathematics. Start focusing early on prime factorization fields, basic combinatorics counting, and Euclidean triangles logic.

⚡ Physics Concepts Suite (Class 9)

Basics (Board Crackers): Lakhmir Singh & Manjit Kaur Class 9. Excellent for visualizing vector kinematics, graphs of motion, and numerical work examples.

Advanced (JEE Foundation): Foundation Science Physics by HC Verma Class 9. Provides deep algebraic derivations for Newton's laws and mechanics variables.

🧪 Chemistry Frameworks (Class 9)

Basics (Board Crackers): S. Chand Class 9 Chemistry. Keeps atom models, electronic configurations, and matter separation concepts highly organized.

Advanced (IIT Foundation): Pearson IIT Foundation Series Chemistry Class 9. Dive early into the mole concept, chemical stoichiometry, and atomic mass math.

🌱 Biology Master Resources (Class 9)

Basics (Board Crackers): Concise Biology Class 9 (Selina) or S. Chand. Clear, scannable breakdowns of cell structures and complex animal/plant tissue groups.

Advanced (Olympiad Foundation): Pearson NEET Foundation Biology Class 9. Builds advanced physiological vocabulary that sets up strong scores for junior biology olympiads.
